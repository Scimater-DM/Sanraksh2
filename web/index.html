IF IT GOES UNCHECKEDPersonal data could leave the approved boundary, depending on provider and retention settings.The person affected may face privacy harm; the organization may need incident review and response.

Why can only an admin add shared documents?

This demo treats uploads as shared team knowledge. Admins assign access roles to reduce accidental oversharing and unreviewed instructions.

Production systems can support personal uploads or approval workflows through the company document platform.
HOW A COMPANY USES IT
1 Staff asks for help with an approved task→2 Sanraksh checks the prompt and document permissions→3 The model drafts, summarizes, or answers from authorized sources→4 The response is checked before it returns
